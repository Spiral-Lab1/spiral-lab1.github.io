About Our Lab

Welcome to the SPIRaL Lab at Emory Rollins School of Public Health. We use wearable sensors as tools to understand how light, temperature, and other environmental exposures influence health in real-world settings.

Current Research Areas

Light Epidemiology

Using data from body-worn sensors to investigate light as an environmental exposure in exposome and public health research.

Temperature and Heat Exposure

Collecting person-based measures of environmental and body temperature to investigate health effects of heat.

Chronohealth in Epilepsy

Evaluating the relationship of environment, rhythms, and sleep to health and quality of life in epilepsy.

Chronohealth in Population-based and Epidemiological Studies

Examining associations of daily behavioral rhythms and shift work to sleep and health.
